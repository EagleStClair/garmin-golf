Riverside 9/17 Round Analysis

# Overall
Front 9 didn't feel spectacular, didn't feel bad just didn't feel I was tracking towards an excellent round likely due to the double bogey on 8.  With that said, zero penalties on Front 9 and limited damage to the one H8 with a double.

- Driver was average. My fade/slice was definitely there, played with it pointing left often with most drives somewhere around 195-210 due to strong fade killing distance. I only had one hole where I got a hold of it proper and sent it straight 245+, I had to focus on turning over wrists to execute that.  Ended up bagging the driver for several holes on back nine where I felt less confident.

- Long clubs -- honestly really good. Not my best, but misses were mainly straight so just some lost distance. My one penalty was on H12 where I simply sent my 5W too straight and it sailed way to far which ended up trees left.  However, I'm proud that even with the drop I was able to save bogey (a first for me). 9W is my favorite -- honestly the most reliable club I have where I always seems to shoot it very well. Directly resulted in my birdie on H18

- Irons -- Really Good.  I think I am starting to really get used to the new irons. Largely true and straight.  I went with 10 yards over my distances registered in The Turn (meaning 7i at 150 instead of 140) and that seemed to work nicely.

- Wedges - Meh, honestly I need a lot of work with these and so I tried to minimize their impact as much as possible here by switching to bump and runs whenever even remotely possible. Lots and lots of pitching wedge chip bumps and that honestly worked pretty well.  A real strength today was that switch.  

- Putting - Excellent -- best I have ever putt with many 1 putts and 32 overall. The main contributor is my switch to trying to hit the back of the hole on 10' minus putts. Putting enough oomf on the ball is a big change for me, so if I miss often 4' passed but that is far better than dribbling in and often left short for me.
